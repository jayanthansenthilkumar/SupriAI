03 / 18 Introduction

Problem & Objective

Modern professionals spend 6+ hours daily in browsers with no insight into productivity patterns, wasted time, or learning opportunities.

The Problem

No Productivity Awareness

Users cannot track or quantify how productively they browse the web

No Anomaly Alerts

Unusual browsing spikes or late-night sessions go unnoticed

No Predictive Insights

Existing tools show past data but cannot forecast future patterns

No Personalized Guidance

No AI-driven focus scheduling or learning content recommendations

Our Objective

Build an AI-Powered Browser Extension

Chrome MV3 extension with a Python ML backend embedding 3 core algorithms

Predict & Forecast Productivity

Random Forest regression, time-series forecasting, and temporal pattern prediction

Detect Anomalies & Classify Content

Isolation Forest anomaly detection and Naive Bayes website classification

Recommend & Personalize

Neural network recommendations, NLP analysis, collaborative filtering, focus scheduling

05 / 18 Literature Survey

Literature Survey I - AI in Adaptive Education

Key research on AI-driven personalized learning, intelligent tutoring systems, and student performance prediction

Paper 11

Artificial Intelligence in Adaptive Education: A Systematic Review of Techniques for Personalized Learning

Hariyanto, F. X. D. Kristianingsih, R. Maharani - 2025

Systematic review of AI-driven adaptive learning technologies. Highlights uses of supervised/unsupervised learning, reinforcement learning, and multimodal analytics to tailor learning experiences. AI significantly enhances personalization but notes challenges in interpretability and privacy, suggesting future work on ethical and inclusive AI systems.

Paper 12

A Comprehensive Review of AI-based Intelligent Tutoring Systems: Applications and Challenges

M. Zerkouk, M. Mihoubi, B. Chikhaoui - 2025

Surveys AI-based intelligent tutoring systems (ITS) from 2010–2025 across pedagogy, NLP, student modeling, and domain-specific ITS. Highlights evolution with natural language and adaptive feedback, but identifies ongoing challenges in evaluation, scalability, and data ethics. Emphasizes improving experimental rigor and transparency.

Paper 18

A Systematic Literature Review of Student Performance Prediction Using Machine Learning Techniques

B. Albreiki, N. Zaki, H. Alashwal - 2021

Reviews 2009–2021 studies on predicting student dropout and at-risk cases via EDM. A variety of ML classifiers (SVM, decision trees, neural nets) significantly improve early identification of struggling students, enabling timely interventions across institutional and online learning datasets.

06 / 18 Literature Survey

Literature Survey II - Privacy & Explainability

Research on federated learning, privacy-preserving personalization, and explainable AI in educational contexts

Paper 21

Revolutionizing AI-Assisted Education with Federated Learning: A Pathway to Privacy-Preserving Learning Ecosystems

A. P. Hridi, R. Sahay, S. Hosseinalipour, B. Akram - 2024

Advocates federated learning (FL) as a solution for training educational AI models on distributed student data without compromising privacy. Discusses benefits (student control, data scarcity) and challenges (heterogeneity, incentives, bias). Outlines open issues in applying FL to education.

Paper 17

Privacy-Preserving Personalization in Education: A Federated Recommender System for Student Performance Prediction

R. Tertulino, R. Almeida - 2025

Introduces a federated learning approach to build a recommender on student performance while keeping data decentralized. Using ASSISTments dataset, FedProx yields more stable accuracy than FedAvg. Achieves F1 of 76.3% (92% of centralized model), illustrating effective personalization without centralizing data.

Paper 19

Evaluating the Explainers: Black-Box Explainable Machine Learning for Student Success Prediction in MOOCs

V. Swamy, B. Radmehr, N. Krco, M. Marras, T. Käser - 2022

Compares five popular post-hoc explanation methods (LIME, SHAP, DiCE, CEM) applied to deep learning student performance models. Different explainers produce very different feature importance rankings, highlighting the need for careful selection and validation of explanation techniques in educational data mining.

08 / 18 Machine Learning

3 Core ML/DL Algorithms

2 Traditional ML + 1 Deep Learning model powering the core browsing intelligence features

01 ML

Productivity Predictor

Random Forest Regressor (100 trees)

Predicts daily productivity score (0–100) with confidence intervals from individual tree variance

23 features: day one-hot (7), hour ratios (4),
category ratios (6), prev-day, rolling 7d score
max_depth=10, feature importance ranking
R² ≈ 0.76+
02 ML

Anomaly Detector

Isolation Forest (contamination=0.1)

Detects unusual browsing patterns — unusual hours, time spikes, session length outliers. Outputs severity: normal → mild → moderate → severe

10 features: total time, category ratios,
peak hour, max single-domain ratio,
session count, category entropy
03 Deep Learning

Learning Content Recommender

Multi-Layer Perceptron Neural Network (MLPClassifier)

Recommends learning content across 12 categories: programming, data science, web dev, cloud, security, design, math, research, languages, business, general, career

18 input→ 128→ 64→ 32→ 12 categories
Activation: ReLU + Adam optimizer
Regularization: L2 (α=0.001) + Early Stopping
Training: 960 samples (80/category)
CV Accuracy ≈ 87%

Model Training

3 core models trained on aggregated data. APScheduler auto-retrains every 6 hours

Prediction & Insights

Real-time predictions served via REST. Popup fetches and renders all 3 model outputs

Model Persistence

All models saved as .pkl files via Joblib. Loaded on startup, retrained periodically

16 / 18 Future Scope

Future Enhancements

Planned improvements and research directions for SupriAI

Advanced Deep Learning

Replace scikit-learn MLPs with PyTorch/TensorFlow models — LSTM for temporal patterns, Transformers for NLP content analysis, and attention-based recommender systems

Cross-Browser Support

Extend to Firefox (WebExtensions API) and Edge with a unified backend. Build a companion mobile app for cross-device sync

Cloud Deployment

Deploy Flask ML backend on AWS/GCP with Docker. Enable multi-user accounts, cloud storage, and scalable model serving via TensorFlow Serving

Team Productivity

Add collaborative team dashboards for organizations — anonymized group analytics, team productivity leaderboards, and shared focus goals

Privacy-First Architecture

Implement federated learning so models train on-device without sending browsing data to servers. Differential privacy for shared analytics

Advanced Visualizations

3D browsing maps with D3.js, interactive heatmaps, Sankey flow diagrams for tab navigation, and exportable PDF reports

Research Directions

Explore reinforcement learning for adaptive focus scheduling, GANs for synthetic browsing data augmentation, and graph neural networks for modeling domain relationships and tab navigation patterns

Natural Language Interface

Voice-powered browsing assistant using Whisper ASR + LLM integration. "What did I spend most time on today?" — answered from ML model outputs directly
